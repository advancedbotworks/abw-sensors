# Support

## Contact us

Email **[support@advancedbotworks.com](mailto:support@advancedbotworks.com)** and include:

- the **product** (e.g. IR Sensor Ring v1) and roughly when you bought it
- the **firmware version** if you can (see [Firmware versions](firmware/versions.md) for how to check)
- your **order number**, if you have it
- **what you expected and what happened**, plus the R/G/B values you're seeing if you can
- a **photo or short video**, if it helps show the problem

We're a small team in Brisbane and usually reply within a few business days.

## Before you email

- [ ] Check that the sensor is plugged firmly into the hub port. Try a different port.
- [ ] Restart the hub and reconnect it to the SPIKE app.
- [ ] Check the sensor shows up as a **Color Sensor** in the app's device view.
- [ ] Read the troubleshooting tips on your product's page.

## Bugs and feature requests

If you've found a bug in the firmware, or have an idea for it, you can also [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/advancedbotworks/abw-sensors/issues).
